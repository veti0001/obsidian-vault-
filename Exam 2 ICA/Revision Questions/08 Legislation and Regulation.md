# Legislation and Regulation — Revision Questions

## Part A — Definitions and Recall
Source: [[Legislation\Appointed Actuary]], [[Foreign Company AA]], [[Policy Holders Dividend]], [[Legislation\Supervision]], [[Regulation\Appointed Actuary Legal Requirements, Qualifications and Peer Review]], [[Memorandum to the Appointed Actuary]]

### A1. Notification of AA appointment (domestic)

When must a company notify the Superintendent after appointing its actuary?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - The company must notify the Superintendent in writing forthwith after the appointment of the actuary.

### A2. CEO/COO appointment as AA

Under what condition can a CEO or COO be appointed as AA?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Only if authorized in writing by the Superintendent.

### A3. CFO as AA

What two conditions must be met for a CFO to be appointed as AA?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - The audit committee provides a written statement that duties will be adequately performed and actuarial duties will be performed independently.
> - The appointment is authorized in writing by the Superintendent.

### A4. Termination of CEO/COO AA authorization

How long can an authorization for CEO/COO to be AA remain in effect?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - For no more than six months from the date issued.

### A5. Circumstances when AA ceases office

List the four circumstances under which a person ceases to hold office as AA.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Resignation as actuary of the company.
> - Ceases to be an actuary.
> - Death.
> - Appointment revoked by directors.

### A6. AA resignation effective date

When does an AA's resignation become effective?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - At the time the written resignation is sent to the company, or at the time specified in the resignation, whichever is later.

### A7. Vacancy in AA office

What must directors do when there is a vacancy in the office of AA?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Notify the Superintendent forthwith and fill the vacancy.

### A8. Exit statement requirement

What must an AA do if they resign or are revoked?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Submit a written statement to directors and the Superintendent setting out circumstances and reasons for resignation or why, in the actuary's opinion, the appointment was revoked.

### A9. Valuation scope for domestic AA

What two items must the AA value under section 365?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Actuarial and other policy liabilities of the company as at end of financial year.
> - Any other matter specified in a direction by the Superintendent.

### A10. Qualified privilege

What protection is given to statements/reports by an AA (or former)?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Any oral or written statement or report made under the Act by the actuary or former actuary has qualified privilege and they are not liable in civil action for such statements/reports made in good faith under specified sections.

### A11. Foreign company AA notification

When must a foreign company notify the Superintendent about AA revocation?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Forthwith after revocation of the foreign company's AA.

### A12. Policyholder dividend - AA role

What must the company's actuary do before directors declare a policyholder dividend?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Report in writing to directors on fairness to participating policyholders of the proposed dividend/bonus/benefit and whether it is in accordance with the dividend/bonus policy.

### A13. Supervision - reserve inclusion

What must be included in annual return liabilities?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - A reserve equal to the value of actuarial and other policy liabilities and other matters determined under section 365 or 629.

### A14. Peer reviewer qualifications (core)

Name two core qualification expectations for an AA under regulation.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Fellowship in the CIA (FICA).
> - Compliance with the CIA's Rules of Professional Conduct and accepted actuarial practice.

### A15. MTA - purpose

What is the main purpose of the Memorandum to the Appointed Actuary?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Describe OSFI expectations regarding the AA's report (AAR) on the valuation of actuarial and other policy liabilities.

## Part B — Explain, Compare, Advantages and Disadvantages
Source: [[Legislation\Appointed Actuary]], [[Foreign Company AA]], [[Policy Holders Dividend]], [[Regulation\Appointed Actuary Legal Requirements, Qualifications and Peer Review]], [[Memorandum to the Appointed Actuary]]

### B1. Explain handover protections

Explain the protections in legislation when a new AA is appointed after resignation/revocation.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - The new AA must request and receive the written statement from the previous AA before accepting appointment (s.364/627).
> - If no reply within fifteen days, the new AA may proceed.
> - If not complied with (and 15-day rule doesn't apply), the appointment is void.

### B2. Compare domestic vs foreign AA valuation scope

Compare the scope of valuation for domestic AA vs foreign company AA.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Domestic AA values actuarial and other policy liabilities of the company as at year-end and other matters specified by direction.
> - Foreign company AA values actuarial and other policy liabilities of the foreign company with respect to its insurance business in Canada as at year-end and other matters specified by direction.

### B3. Explain "qualified privilege"

Explain what qualified privilege means for the AA and its practical effect.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Oral/written statements/reports made under the Act have qualified privilege.
> - In good faith, the AA/former AA is not liable in civil action for such statements/reports.

### B4. Advantages of peer review

List two advantages of actuarial peer review as described in regulation.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Contributes to safety and soundness of insurance companies.
> - Provides independent advice, consultation, and professional education to the AA.

### B5. Disadvantages/constraints on selecting peer reviewer

List two constraints on who may serve as peer reviewer.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Cannot be an employee of the company or affiliates, and cannot have been employed or served as AA in the last three years.
> - Cannot have a direct financial interest in the company (other than policyholder/depositor/beneficiary/insured).

## Part C — Application and Scenarios
Source: [[Legislation\Appointed Actuary]], [[Foreign Company AA]], [[Policy Holders Dividend]], [[Legislation\Supervision]], [[Regulation\Appointed Actuary Legal Requirements, Qualifications and Peer Review]]

### C1. Scenario: AA certificate lapses in effect? (resignation timing)

Scenario: An AA sends a written resignation letter to the company on March 5 and specifies it takes effect March 20. When does the AA cease office?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - March 20 (later of sending date or specified date).

### C2. Scenario: Foreign insurer appoints resident AA

Scenario: A foreign insurer operating in Canada appoints a new AA for its Canadian business. What must the foreign company do forthwith regarding notification?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Notify the Superintendent in writing forthwith after the appointment (consistent with domestic requirement context) and if vacancy occurs, notify and fill.

### C3. Scenario: P&C declares policyholder dividend

Scenario: A P&C insurer with participating policies wants to declare a policyholder dividend. What step involving AA is required before declaration?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - The company's actuary must report in writing to directors on fairness and compliance with dividend/bonus policy; directors must consider the report.

### C4. Scenario: New AA discovers exit statement not received

Scenario: A company revokes its AA. A prospective AA requests the exit statement but receives no reply after 14 days. Can they accept appointment on day 15?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - No, they may only accept after 15 days have elapsed with no reply (must wait 15 full days).

### C5. Scenario: Material adverse finding to AA

Scenario: A potential AA has an adverse finding from CIA Disciplinary Tribunal. Is appointment automatically barred?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - No; Superintendent may conclude the AA is suitable if circumstances and other information support such conclusion.

## Part D — Exam-Style Questions
Source: [[Legislation\Appointed Actuary]], [[Foreign Company AA]], [[Regulation\Appointed Actuary Legal Requirements, Qualifications and Peer Review]], [[Memorandum to the Appointed Actuary]], [[Policy Holders Dividend]]

### D1. Dual role conflict (CFO as AA)

Discuss the legislative/regulatory safeguards when a person serves as both CFO and AA of a company. What approvals/statements are required?

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Audit committee must provide written statement indicating duties adequately performed and actuarial duties performed independently.
> - Appointment/holding must be authorized by Superintendent.
> - Authorization may include limitations/conditions including time limit; person may not hold after limit.

### D2. Independence and objectivity in peer review

Explain the requirements for selecting a peer reviewer to ensure objectivity, including firm-level considerations.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - No employee/affiliate, no prior employment as AA in last three years.
> - No direct financial interest (except policyholder/depositor/beneficiary/insured).
> - If consulting firm has AA, another member cannot be peer reviewer unless no involvement in actuarial work for the company.
> - Peer reviewer changes every six years.

### D3. Reporting obligations on financial condition

Outline the AA's reporting obligations when material adverse effects on financial condition are identified (domestic).

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Report in writing to CEO and CFO.
> - Forthwith provide copy to directors.
> - If suitable action not taken, send copy to Superintendent and advise directors.

### D4. AAR content expectations (MTA)

List four required elements in the Appointed Actuary's Report as per the MTA and briefly explain each.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - Expression of opinion: on policy liabilities in financial statements per ICA, using CIA format.
> - Changes influencing valuation: external/internal environment, assumptions/methods, other changes.
> - Data: description, notable changes from prior year and reasons.
> - Assumptions/methodology/reliance: describe key assumptions, methods, and reliance on others.

### D5. Valuation governance and Superintendent powers

Describe Superintendent powers related to valuation and expense recovery.

> [!answer]- Answer
> - May appoint an independent actuary to value matters if necessary (not the company AA/foreign AA).
> - Expenses incurred are payable by the company/foreign company on Superintendent's written approval.
> - May issue directions changing generally accepted actuarial practice for valuation.
