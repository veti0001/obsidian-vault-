# Revision Questions — Index

320 self-test questions generated from the notes in `New Notes`, organised by section. Every question is answerable strictly from your own notes, and each answer sits in a collapsed callout so you can commit to an answer before revealing it.

## How to use these

- Cover the answer, write your response first, then expand the callout. Recall beats recognition, so the value is in the attempt.
- Part A is fast recall — good for a fast warm-up.
- Part B is where most exam marks sit: explaining, comparing, and justifying.
- Part C is numeric practice (or scenarios, for the standards sections).
- Part D is full essay practice — attempt these under timed conditions.
- Anything you get wrong, fix by re-reading the linked source note, not the answer key.

## Suggested order

1. [[09 General and Ultimate Claims]] and [[03 Data]] — foundational, small and quick.
2. [[01 Reserving Techniques]] and [[02 Advanced Reserving]] — the largest technical block.
3. [[04 IFRS 17]] — the most formulaic; grind through Part C.
4. [[07 Professional Requirements]] — mixed calculation and judgement.
5. [[05 ICA Standards of Practice - The Work]] and [[06 ICA Standards of Practice - Reporting and Valuation]] — learn by scenario, not by rote.
6. [[08 Legislation and Regulation]] — best done last, close to the exam, since it is pure recall.
