## Selecting Exposure Basis

- Need to be careful when selecting an exposure basis
- Multiple criteria need to be taken into account

## Aggregation of Exposure Data

### Calendar Year

- **Definition:** All transactions that happen during a year
- **Characteristics:** CY premiums are fixed at the end of the year
- **Used with:**
    - Claims aggregated by accident year
    - Claims aggregated by report year
- EP x = WP x + UEP (x -1) -  UEP x

### Policy Year

- **Definition:** All policies that are written during a year are aggregated together
- **Characteristics:** Policy year extends for two calendar years
- **Used with:**
    - Claims aggregated by policy year
- EP x = WP x - UEP x
