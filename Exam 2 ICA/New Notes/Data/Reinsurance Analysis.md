## Usefulness |

- Companies need to report results gross and net of reinsurance
- **Gross of reinsurance:**
    - Includes assumed reinsurance
- **Net of reinsurance:**
    - Cessions to reinsurers are removed from the data

## Possible Analysis |

- Separately analyze claims on a direct, assumed, and ceded basis
- Analyze claims on a gross and net basis:
    - Net claims = direct + assumed - ceded
- Choices are made based on:
    - The insurer's reinsurance program
    - Type of data segregation available
    - Volume of data
    - Impact of reinsurance on claim experience
    - Professional judgement
    - Standards
**Actuarial Implication:** Gross triangles support pricing and reinsurance negotiation; net triangles support reserve adequacy and financial reporting.

## Reinsurance Usage

- Increase capacity
- Provide Catastrophe protection
- Stabilize claim experience
- Offer technical service and expertise
- Strengthen primary insurer financial position and provide capital relief
- Facilitate withdrawals from a market segment 
## Type of Reinsurance

### Pro rata reinsurance

- #### Quota Share
	- share a fixed percentage of policy, premium and claims
- #### Surplus Share
	- shared percentage varies by type of risk and policy limits

### Excess of loss reinsurance 

- Reinsurer pays claims over a specified threshold (attachment point)

### Aggregate stop loss

- all claims over a specified amount are transferred to the reinsurer

## Risk attaching vs Loss-Occuring

- Risk attaching:
	- Cover all reinsurance contracts that are incepted during the reinsurance contract term
	- Premiums are accounted for over 2 years
	
- Loss-Occuring
	- Cover loss that arrive during the period 
	- Premiums are accounted for over 1 year
