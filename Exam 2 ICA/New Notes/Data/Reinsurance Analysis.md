## Usefulness |

- Companies need to report results gross and net of reinsurance
- **Gross of reinsurance:**
    - Includes assumed reinsurance
- **Net of reinsurance:**
    - Cessions to reinsurers are removed from the data

## Possible Analysis |

- Separately analyze claims on a direct, assumed, and ceded basis
- Analyze claims on a gross and net basis:
    - Net claims = direct + assumed - ceded
- Choices are made based on:
    - The insurer's reinsurance program
    - Type of data segregation available
    - Volume of data
    - Impact of reinsurance on claim experience
    - Professional judgement
    - Standards
**Actuarial Implication:** Gross triangles support pricing and reinsurance negotiation; net triangles support reserve adequacy and financial reporting.
