## Definition

- Risk not specifically considered when the policy was written
- Types:
	-  Bodily injury
	- Pharmaceutical claims
	- Environmental damage
## How to determine Reserve

- Top down:
	- consider market wide losses
	- ignores all the differences between the different insurer
- Bottom up:
	- Review every policies to see if they can be affected by the latent claims
- Future:
	- once the latent claims have happened the insurer will change pricing or change policy wording
	- There should not be any more issue
