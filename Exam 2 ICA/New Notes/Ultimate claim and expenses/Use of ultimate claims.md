
## Financial Reporting
- Liability values need to be estimated in financial reports to be in line with IFRS 17.
- Appointed Actuaries (AA) need to disclose their opinion on the adequacy of the reserve amount.
- Incurred claims are needed to derive underwriting (UW) income.
- Helps prepare financial statement disclosures.

## Pricing
- Ultimate claims for past periods are needed to set rate levels.

## Financial Condition Analysis
- Tests the ability of an insurer to meet its obligations if an extreme event happens.
- Exam 3 content.

## Planning
- Ultimate claim values are used for planning and budgeting purposes.
- Needed to know how to allocate resources (financial and non-financial).
- Ultimate claims are also needed to determine employee bonuses.

## Merger and Acquisition Analyses
- Needed to know the actual value of the company.
