- **Average Values**:
    
    - Average paid: Paid claims / closed count
    - Average paid on closed: Paid claims on closed files / count closed with payment
    - Average reported: Reported claim / reported count
	    - Value will change between each column based on the trend
	    - Change can happened in numerator and denominator
    - Average reported on counts: Excluding count closed with no payment (CNP count) and open counts with no payments or case estimates
    - Average case estimates: Case estimates divided by open counts
	    - (Reported Claims - Paid Claims) * 1000 / (reported count - closed count)
	    - used to see if there is any changes in the adequacy of case estimate reserve (need to consider large claims)

- **Ratios of Indemnity, ALAE, or Claims**:
    
    - Ratio of paid to reported
	    -  Initial test to see if there is any changes in the settlement patterns or adequacy of case estimates over the exposure period.
	    - One of the most calculated triangle analysis metric
	    - Change in the numerator could be affected or offset by changes in the denominator
    - Ratio of indemnity paid on closed counts to total paid
    - Ratio of net of reinsurance to gross of reinsurance (reported or paid)
    - Ratio at basic limit or total limit (reported or paid)
- **Ratios of Counts**:
    
    - Closed to reported counts
	    - frequently used with ratio of paid to reported count to determine if similar pattern exist between the count and the amounts
    - Count closed with no payment to total closed counts
	    - is used to gather further insights about potential changes in settlement processes that could potentially affect the applicability of using historical paid claims to project ultimate claims 
    - Count closed with payment to reported counts excluding count closed with no payment
    - Open to reported count
- **Ratios of ALAE and S&S**:
    
    - Paid ALAE to paid claims
    - Reported ALAE to reported claims
    - Received S&S to paid claims
    - Reported S&S to reported claims
- **Ratios Using Exposures**:
    
    - Ratio of reported claims to earned premiums
    - Ratio of reported claims to on-level earned premiums
    - Ratio of reported claims to earned exposures
    - Ratio of reported counts to earned exposures

## **What can cause difference in values**

### Reported Claims

- New procedures for the payment of claims such as direct deposit to a claimant's bank account instead of issuance of checks;
- New philosophies about establishment of case estimates such as explicit consideration of S&S recoveries;
- Changes in the distribution of policy limits purchased by insureds;
- Changes in the use of partial settlements or ex gratia payments; and
- Shifts in the attitude toward defense of questionable claim files.

### Reported Count

- A new claim management IT system being put into service;
- A call center system being instituted for the handling of claims; and
- A modification to the definition of a count; for example all incidents are now recorded as counts in the claim management system.
