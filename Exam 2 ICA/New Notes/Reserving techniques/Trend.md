## Goal

- The goal is to adjust historical claim experience to a cost level associated with a common point in time
- Takes into account :
	- economic
	- societal 
	- demographic forces
	- regulatory and legal

## How

- look at trend in ult frequency, ult severity and ult pure premium

## Factors that will influence Frequency and Severity

- Deductible
	- Shift in average deductible will affect:
		- Freq (claims under deductible will not happen anymore)
		- Severity (increasing deductible  = increase severity in general if heavy tail)
- Policy limits
	- Higher deductible = Higher Severity and Higher trend in Severity
- Coverages
	- depends on what happens 
- Other characteristics affecting the mix of business in the portfolio

## Trending procedures

- Seek a balance between responsiveness and stability
	- Need a sufficient volume of credible experience form a long enough period of time

## Type of data used

- Same data has the one used to project ultimate claims
- Need ultimate projected values
- Need to balance stability (longer period of data) with responsiveness (shorter period of data)
- Short tail can assign more weight to recent years has there is more certainty around ultimate claim estimate (4 to 5 years)
- For long tail short and long time (8 to 10 years) period is considered while calculating trend
- Might need to remove more immature year has the claim uncertainty is really high
- Might need to remove historical years if there were majors changes that might affect the trends
- Might need to remove outliers
- Need to consider when selecting data point :
	- relevance of the historical experience
	- reliability of the recent data points 
	- purpose for witch the trend will be used 
	- goodness of fit statistics from the regression analysis
- Data would need to be adjusted for the following:
	- Large claims 
	- Large number of large claims 
	- Catastrophe claims 
	- Seasonality
	- Tort reform
	- Legislated benefits level changes
