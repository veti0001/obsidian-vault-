## Assumptions 

- Emergence can be forecasted with reasonable accuracy
- Claim file that are closed at each age are a function of the number of claims files to be closed in the future
- Average closure amounts at each age are of the averages for preceding accident periods
- Inflation influences the cost at the time the claim is closed

## Data Requirements

- Estimate of ultimate count 
- development triangles (incremental) for closed counts and paid claims
- trend assumptions
