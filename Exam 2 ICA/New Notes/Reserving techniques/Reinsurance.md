## Possible analysis

Two approaches:
- Project ultimate claims **gross** and **net**, then derive ceded ultimate as the difference.
- Project ultimate **ceded** claims directly.
### Quota Share
- Apply ceded percentage directly to ultimate claims.

### Excess of Loss
Analysis depends on:
- Volume of available data.
- Changes in attachment points or limits.
Possible approaches:
- Select development factors **gross** of reinsurance and apply them to **net** claim data.
- do a separate analysis for ceded and gross claims

## Ratio technical approach

- CDF at limit L = CDF ult X (Ratio severity at n /Ratio Severity at t) 
	- for the severity ratio we divide severity at limit L by unlimited severity
- CDF at excess = CDF ult X (( 1- Ratio severity at n) / (1 - Ratio Severity at t))
