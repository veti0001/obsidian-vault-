### Understand differences in Ultimate Values

- Need to understand while different method produce different estimate
- can be due to:
	- Unusually high of low value for paid or reported claims at the valuation date
	- Highly leverage factor
	- Assumptions of specific method that are violated
	- Sensitivity to the difference method to the assumptions or claims experience

### Factor to check

- We need to be confident in the following factor to be able to select a method to project ultimate claims :
	- Development factor
	- Trend factor 
	- Premium on-level factor
	- Tort reform factor
	- Expected claim ratio or pure premium
	- Frequencies and Sevrities
