## Chain ladder

#### Advantages
- most basic method
- input to many other methods
#### Disadvantages
- very susceptible to change in book of business
- sensitive to highly leverage DF (more immature accident year)
## Expected Claim

#### Advantages
- Stability
#### Disadvantages
- lack of responsiveness (too stable)
## BF

#### Advantages
- More responsive to actual claims then the development method and more stable
- Estimate will not be distorted by random fluctuation at early maturities as the development method is
#### Disadvantages
- Claim ratio not accurately estimated = IBNR inacurate

## Cape Cod

#### Advantages
- The estimate produce by the metho is always the same if the data is the same
- use claims in the determination of ECR so respond partially to changes in it
- more stable then Chain Ladder
#### Disadvantages
- No professional judgement possible (could be good or bad thing really), so not good if data is really sparse or volatile
- Dependent on the on-level factor

## Freq Sev

#### Advantages
- Gain valuable info on claim process
- Better application of trends
#### Disadvantages
- Estimate really sensible to selected trends
