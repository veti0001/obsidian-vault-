## Definition

- Omission or misstatement is material if the judgment of a reasonable person would have been changed or influenced by the inclusion or correction of the item, given surrounding circumstances.
- user is key to determination of materiality
- Only need to consider intended audience when determining materiality threshold

## Consideration when determining materiality

- Depends on the needs, skill, sophistication, and experience of the intended audience for the actuary’s work
- Task-specific and not product specific
- Materiality threshold should not change in time
- Materiality threshold varies in function of the following:
	- Size of the entity;
	- Entity’s access to capital;
	- Stage of organizational life cycle; 
	- Type of business (e.g., multi-line vs. single line, personal lines vs. commercial lines); 
	- Net retention
## Disclosure

- Materiality should not be disclose if it would create misunderstanding and confusion from the intended user
- The purpose of the work should be adequately define in the work so that it cant be misused (mainly due to materiality level)
