## Requirements

- Include all future cash flows within the contract boundary, including those that may have been previously accounted for separately from the insurance contract liability (e.g., overdue premiums);
- Are estimates of the probability weighted mean of the full range of possible outcomes;
- Are unbiased (i.e., they do not include the risk adjustment for non-financial risk);
- Reflect the perspective of the entity rather than a market perspective (except that estimates of market variables are consistent with observable market values for those variables);
- Are current; and
- Are explicit.

## Cash flow include:

- Premiums, including overdue premiums;
- Claims that have been reported but not yet paid;
- Incurred claims that have not yet been reported;
- Payments on future claims on unexpired risks;
- Premiums, including overdue premiums;
- An allocation of insurance acquisition cash flows;
- Claim handling costs;
- Costs the entity will incur for payments in kind;
- Policy administration and maintenance costs;
- Transaction-based costs such as premium taxes and levies;
- Income taxes either paid in a fiduciary capacity to meet obligations of the policyholder or specifically chargeable to the policyholder;
- Cash inflows from salvage, subrogation, and other recoveries;
- Retrospective premium adjustments based on claim experience within the contract boundary
- An allocation of fixed and variable overheads directly attributable to fulfilling insurance contracts;

## Aggregation of cash flows

- Cash flows can usually be identified at the individual contract level.
- Cash flow can be determine at whatever level but must be allocated to groups of insurance contracts

## Definition of unbiased

- Capture information about the full range of possible outcomes
- Should not have an intention of attaining a particular outcome, and
 - Should not have an intention of influencing a particular behavior.
 - There is no positivism of conservatism in the CF

## Cash inflow

- Premium
- Investment income, other than that related to policy loans, **is not included**
- Salvage & Subrogation

## Cash outflow

- Benefit or claim payments
- directly related expenses, and similar items
- If the expense cannot be directly attributed to the portfolio than it will not be included
