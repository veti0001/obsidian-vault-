## Usefulness

- Simplification of the calculation needed for LRC
- LIC is really similar to GMA

## Criteria to choose PAA vs GMA

- extent to which existing and potential future contracts are eligible for the PAA
- extent to which existing systems and processes support reporting the PAA
- The additional resources and costs that may be required to implement the GMA compared with PAA

## Advantages

- Simple from an operational standpoint as we do not need to apply discounting or apply a CSM/LC.
- Also has better alignment with current/prior practices, allowing for an easier transition from pre IFRS17 account to IFRS17

## Disadvantages

- It is limited in applicability as it is not suitable for longer contract lengths and longer tailed business.
- It also has reduced transparency as there are less disclosures about future profitability and cash flow timing.
