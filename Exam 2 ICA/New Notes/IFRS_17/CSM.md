## Definition

- A component of the carrying amount of the asset or liability for a group of insurance contracts representing the unearned profit the entity will recognize as it provides insurance contract services under the insurance contracts in the group.
## Purpose

- Only in GMA
- represents the unearned profit the entity will recognize as it provides services
- component of the insurance contract liability for a group
- recognize gradually over time in line with the services provided
- if contract are onerous no CSM, it will be replace by LC
- The CSM reflects the IASB’s view that profit on insurance contracts should only be recognized as service is provided

## Determination at initial recognition

- CSM = total cash flow - total cash inflow (should be negative if contact is not onerous)
