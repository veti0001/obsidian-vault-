
## PAA

- Disclose of witch threshold a contract has met to be PAA eligible
- Disclose witch judgements that management has made in applying accounting policies that have the most significant effect on the amounts recognized in the financial statements
- You must disclose **significant judgments** used in determining PAA eligibility (e.g., materiality thresholds, grouping decisions, assumptions about variability)

## Reinsurance

- **Judgments, assumptions, and methodologies** used in applying IFRS 17 to reinsurance contracts issued and held.
- **Information relevant to recognition, measurement, and presentation**, including how reinsurance affects the insurance service result, LRC/ARC, RA, and FCF.
- **Contract‑specific features** (e.g., reinstatements, commissions, NDIC, risk of non‑performance) to the extent they materially affect reported amounts.

## RA

- Disclose confidence level of the RA
	- the **confidence level used**, or
	- the **confidence level corresponding to the technique**
- Disclose the approach used to determine RA
	- the **approach** (quantile, CoC, margin, hybrid)
	- the **method** (e.g., VaR, CTE, capital formula, margins by line)
	- how diversification is reflected
	- how reinsurance RA is determine
- Disclose movement of the RA between opening and closing balance
- Disclose significant judgement and change in judgements used in the RA  calculations
	- You must disclose:
		- key assumptions
		- changes in assumptions
		- rationale for method selection
		- rationale for diversification treatment
		- rationale for discounting (if used)

## Discount Rate Disclosure

### Approach and adjustments

- Whether a **bottom-up** or **top-down** approach was used
- How **illiquidity premiums** were derived
- How **credit risk adjustments** were determined
- How **market risk and other adjustments** were handled

### Discount curves

- **Within the observable period** — what discount rates/curves were used for durations where market data exists
- **Beyond the observable period** — how the entity derived long-term (ultimate) rates when market data is no longer observable

### Suggested disclosures when using the published CIA reference curves

- Appropriateness of risk-free rates
- Appropriateness of reference portfolio
- Appropriateness of illiquidity premium assumptions
- Any deviations from published curves
- Materiality considerations

### Illiquidity premium

- How liquidity characteristics were assessed
- Whether LRC and LIC use different illiquidity premiums
- Whether a **single illiquidity premium curve** was applied across liabilities
- Composition of the reference portfolio
- Adjustments made to remove credit or market risk
- How the illiquidity premium was mapped from assets to liabilities

### Top-down approach

- **Yield curve inputs:** disclose the specific risk-free curve used and the rationale for its selection. The CIA requires the curve to be appropriate and consistent with market data
- **Illiquidity premium methodology:** disclose the methodology and inputs used to derive the 0.6% IP. The CIA educational note *Discounting under IFRS 17* discusses the high degree of judgment in this estimate
- **Liability-based IP rationale:** disclose that the IP reflects the illiquid characteristics of the insurance liabilities, not the entity's assets. CIA guidance emphasizes this distinction
- **Judgment and sensitivity:** disclose the significant judgment applied in determining the discount rates, and a sensitivity analysis showing the impact of reasonably possible changes in the discount curve on net income and equity. This aligns with CIA regarding disclosure of significant assumptions and uncertainty

## Accounting disclosure

- Discount Rate selection - Determining what method was used and the basis of either reference portfolios or liquidity premiums
- Expected claims pattern - Timing and rate of incurred losses
- Acquisition Cost Amortization Pattern - How costs were deferred and schedule of amortization
- Risk Adjustment Calculation - What method was used, confidence level and any diversification benefits applied

## Significant judgement needed for

- Portfolio Selection and Grouping - Judgmental selection on which contracts share similar risks and management. Must disclose process for the selection 
- Materiality for PAA vs GMA - Determining the threshold at which results materially differ between the 2 methods. Must disclose why one method was selected over the other
- Discount Rate Selection - Judgmental selection on liquidity premium or removal of risks that do not affect the insurance contract. Disclosure also requires methodology and how non-insurance effects were considered along with any simplifications applied
- Onerous Contract Testing - Judging whether expected future losses exceed the LRC. Must disclose assumptions and how testing affects the grouping and calculation of the LRC
- Contract boundaries
