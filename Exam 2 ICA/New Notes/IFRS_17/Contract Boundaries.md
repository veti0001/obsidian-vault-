## Core Principle

Cash flows belong **within the contract boundary** when they arise from **substantive rights and obligations** that exist during periods in which:

- The entity can **compel the policyholder to pay premiums**, **or**
    
- The entity has a **substantive obligation** to provide **insurance contract services**.
    

## When the Substantive Obligation Ends

### 1. **Individual‑Level Reassessment**

The obligation ends when the entity has the **practical ability to reassess the risks of the particular policyholder** and can set a **price or benefit level** that fully reflects those risks.

### 2. **Portfolio‑Level Reassessment**

The obligation also ends when **both** of the following are satisfied:

- The entity has the **practical ability to reassess the risks of the portfolio** containing the contract and can set a **price or benefit level** that fully reflects the risk of that portfolio.
    
- **Premiums priced up to the reassessment date** do **not** take into account risks relating to periods **after** the reassessment date.
    

## General Boundary

In practice, contract boundaries are typically delineated by:

- **Initial recognition date**, and
    
- **Contract expiry date**.
