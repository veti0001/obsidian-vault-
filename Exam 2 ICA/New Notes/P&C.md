# **Quick Summary**

P&C exhibits form a hierarchical reporting structure for IFRS 17:
- **P&C‑1**: Top‑level aggregation (Statement of Financial Position, P&L, Capital & Solvency)
- **P&C‑2**: Claims development triangles → feeds **LIC** (Liability for Incurred Claims) in P&C‑1
- **P&C‑3**: Premium/exposure detail → feeds **LRC** (Liability for Remaining Coverage) in P&C‑1

Key IFRS 17 linkages:
- **LRC** ← Premium + exposure from P&C‑3
- **LIC** ← Best estimate + RA + discounting from P&C‑2
- **Insurance Revenue** ← Coverage units (P&C‑3) + claims emergence (P&C‑2)
- **Reinsurance Held** ← Ceded balances from both P&C‑2 (claims) and P&C‑3 (premium)

# **1. Summary of P&C‑1, P&C‑2, P&C‑3 (Exam‑Ready)**

## **P&C‑1 — Financial Statements & Key Exhibits**

**Role:** High‑level view of the insurer’s financial position and performance under IFRS 17.

**Contents:**

- **Statement of Financial Position**
    
    - Assets
        
    - Liabilities including **LIC** (Liability for Incurred Claims) and **LRC** (Liability for Remaining Coverage)
        
- **Statement of Profit or Loss**
    
    - Insurance revenue
        
    - Insurance service result
        
    - Insurance finance income/expense
        
- **Capital & Solvency Exhibits**
    
    - MCT components
        
    - Available vs required capital
        
- **Reinsurance Held Summary**
    
    - Gross, ceded, net balances
        

**Exam angle (B2):**

- P&C‑1 is the **top‑level aggregation** of all IFRS 17 balances.
    
- Must **reconcile** to P&C‑2 (claims) and P&C‑3 (premium/exposure).
    

## **P&C‑2 — Claims Development & Unpaid Claims**

**Role:** Detailed view of claims emergence and unpaid claims supporting LIC.

**Contents:**

- **Development triangles**
    
    - Paid
        
    - Incurred
        
    - Counts
        
    - ALAE / ULAE
        
- **Unpaid claims analysis**
    
    - Best estimate
        
    - Discounting
        
    - Risk adjustment
        
    - Reinsurance recoveries
        
- **Large losses & catastrophe claims**
    
- **Layered reinsurance impacts**
    

**Exam angle (B2):**

- P&C‑2 is the **primary support** for the Appointed Actuary’s LIC estimate.
    
- Triangle‑based reserving → IFRS 17 LIC reported in P&C‑1.
    

## **P&C‑3 — Premiums, Exposures & Underwriting Detail**

**Role:** Detailed view of written/earned premium and exposure supporting LRC and revenue.

**Contents:**

- **Written & earned premium** by line and province
    
- **Exposure measures**
    
    - Policy counts
        
    - Sums insured
        
    - Risk units
        
- **Reinsurance ceded premium & commissions**
    
- **Rate changes & mix of business**
    

**Exam angle (B2):**

- P&C‑3 explains the **volume and nature of contracts** generating LRC.
    
- Earned premium must **reconcile** to P&C‑1.
    
- Exposure patterns explain changes in P&C‑2 development.
    

# **2. Mapping P&C‑1/2/3 to IFRS 17 Concepts**

## **P&C‑1 ↔ IFRS 17 Measurement**

- **LRC (Liability for Remaining Coverage)**
    
    - Reported in P&C‑1.
        
    - **Fed by:** premium + exposure from **P&C‑3**.
        
- **LIC (Liability for Incurred Claims)**
    
    - Reported in P&C‑1.
        
    - **Fed by:** best estimate + RA + discounting from **P&C‑2**.
        
- **Risk Adjustment**
    
    - Explicitly disclosed in P&C‑1.
        
    - Derived from uncertainty observed in P&C‑2.
        
- **Insurance Revenue**
    
    - Driven by **coverage units** (P&C‑3) and **claims emergence** (P&C‑2).
        

## **P&C‑2 ↔ IFRS 17 LIC & Risk Adjustment**

- **Best estimate of cash flows**
    
    - Derived from triangles and analyses in P&C‑2.
        
- **Discounting**
    
    - P&C‑2 provides discounted vs undiscounted views → LIC in P&C‑1.
        
- **Risk Adjustment**
    
    - Based on volatility, tail risk, cat exposure, reinsurance structure.
        
- **Reinsurance Held**
    
    - Gross/ceded/net claims → reinsurance held measurement under IFRS 17.
        

## **P&C‑3 ↔ IFRS 17 LRC & Coverage Units**

- **Coverage Units**
    
    - Exposure measures (policy counts, sums insured, risk units) → LRC release pattern.
        
- **Expected Cash Inflows**
    
    - Written premium + expected future premium → fulfilment cash flows for LRC.
        
- **Revenue Recognition**
    
    - Earned premium + exposure patterns → insurance revenue in P&C‑1.
        
- **Reinsurance Held (Premium Side)**
    
    - Ceded premium + commissions → reinsurance held LRC.
